# Calendar dependencies

The community calendar loads pinned standard libraries from jsDelivr when a resident opens Calendar. Their distributed files retain their upstream license headers.

- FullCalendar Standard 6.1.19 and @fullcalendar/luxon3 6.1.19 — MIT license, copyright FullCalendar LLC. https://github.com/fullcalendar/fullcalendar/blob/v6.1.19/LICENSE.md
- Luxon 3.7.2 — MIT license, copyright JS Foundation and other contributors. https://github.com/moment/luxon/blob/3.7.2/LICENSE.md

No FullCalendar Premium plugins are used. See https://fullcalendar.io/license for the distinction between Standard and Premium.
