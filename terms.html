Terms of use

Updated October 13, 2026

Neighborhood Connect is a private resident directory. Use it to connect with your neighborhood, provide accurate household information, and respect other residents' privacy. Access requires approval or a household invitation. Household management and neighborhood administration are separate permissions.

Upload only photos and other content you have permission to share. Do not redistribute residents' contact information or photos without their permission. Neighborhood administrators may remove access when a resident moves, an account is misused, or neighborhood eligibility changes.

Google Street View images may be outdated, unavailable, or show a neighboring property. They are an optional visual aid, not verification of an address or property condition. Uploaded photos take priority in automatic mode, and household managers can hide house images. Keep all Google attribution visible.

Google Maps features are subject to Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Contact your neighborhood administrator for assistance with household membership, profile information, or appropriate use of the directory.
