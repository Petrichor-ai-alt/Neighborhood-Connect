Privacy policy

Updated October 13, 2026

Neighborhood Connect stores account details, resident access requests, household information, member profiles, photo preferences, and uploaded photos using Supabase. Your login email is used for authentication; contact information entered in a profile is visible to approved residents of that neighborhood.

The resident directory and uploaded photos require approved neighborhood access. Neighborhood administrators review access requests. Household managers maintain shared household details and profiles without separate logins. People with their own logins maintain their personal profiles.

Google Maps provides the neighborhood map. If Street View is used, your browser sends the household address and standard request information, including your IP address, to Google to display imagery. Google imagery is displayed directly and is not copied into our photo storage. A household manager can select an uploaded photo or hide house images.

Supabase handles authentication and data storage. Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and the Supabase browser library. These services receive standard browser request information. Invitation codes can be held temporarily in your browser's session storage while you sign in.

Contact your neighborhood administrator to request correction, access removal, or deletion of records and uploaded photos. Removing a login does not automatically delete its member profile. Previously downloaded information cannot be recalled; signed photo links expire within five minutes.

Google services are also subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
